Shinanigans

/shin-AN-i-gunz/ · noun

Playful mischief, but make it code.

Our name is a twist on “shenanigans”: harmless fun, clever tricks and a little chaos. We swapped in an i for ideas, because every project we build starts as a silly idea that somehow ends up working.

Four students, four skills (design, code, web and security), one squad turning a little chaos into real projects.
